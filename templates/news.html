Source Rules

Priority sources
India Meteorological Department (IMD), NDMA, state disaster-management authorities, district administrations and other authoritative government sources.

Grounding
ARAN AI preserves the original source meaning and separates verified information from AI-generated explanation.

No invented warnings
ARAN AI does not create evacuation orders, road closures, cyclone coordinates or government instructions.

Unverified information
Information that cannot be verified should be explicitly labelled UNVERIFIED .
